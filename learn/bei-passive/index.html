被How to Use 被 for the Passive

被 (bèi) makes a passive sentence: the person or thing that something happened to comes first, and the one who did it comes after 被. It is mostly used for things that went wrong or were unexpected, and it is a key grammar point of HSK 3.

The pattern

Receiver + 被 + doer + verb + something else.

Start with the thing the action happened to, then 被, then who did it, then the verb. 我的手机被弟弟用坏了 tells the same story as 弟弟把我的手机用坏了, but it starts from my phone and what happened to it, not from my brother.

  1. 我的手机被弟弟用坏了。Wǒ de shǒujī bèi dìdi yònghuài le.My phone got broken by my little brother.
  2. 蛋糕被妹妹吃了。Dàngāo bèi mèimei chī le.The cake was eaten by my little sister.
  3. 这件事被老师知道了。Zhè jiàn shì bèi lǎoshī zhīdào le.The teacher found out about this.

Leaving out the doer

If you don't know or don't need to say who did it, put the verb straight after 被. To say "someone", use 被人.

  1. 我的钱包被人拿走了。Wǒ de qiánbāo bèi rén názǒu le.Someone took my wallet.
  2. 那本书被借走了。Nà běn shū bèi jièzǒu le.That book has been borrowed.
  3. 他的自行车被人骑走了。Tā de zìxíngchē bèi rén qízǒu le.Someone rode off on his bike.

The verb needs something after it

As with 把, the verb in a 被 sentence can't stand alone. After it, add a result such as 坏 or 完, a direction such as 走 or 回家, or at least 了. 我的手机被弟弟用 is not a complete sentence.

English uses the passive far more often than Chinese. 被 is mostly for things that happened to someone or something and were bad, unwanted or unexpected. When it is obvious that the subject can't do the action itself, Chinese usually leaves 被 out: say 作业做完了, not 作业被做完了.

  1. 他被妈妈叫回家了。Tā bèi māma jiào huí jiā le.His mum called him home.
  2. 鱼被猫吃了。Yú bèi māo chī le.The cat ate the fish.
  3. 作业做完了。Zuòyè zuòwán le.The homework is done.

Negatives, questions and 让

To say something didn't happen, put 没有 before 被, not before the verb, and leave out 了. To warn someone, put 别 before 被. To ask who did it, put 谁 after 被.

In everyday speech, 让 can take the place of 被, but then you must say who did it.

  1. 我的电脑没有被他用坏。Wǒ de diànnǎo méiyǒu bèi tā yònghuài.He didn't break my computer.
  2. 别被他看见。Bié bèi tā kànjiàn.Don't let him see you.
  3. 你的手机被谁拿走了？Nǐ de shǒujī bèi shéi názǒu le?Who took your phone?
  4. 我的蛋糕让弟弟吃了。Wǒ de dàngāo ràng dìdi chī le.My little brother ate my cake.
