再再: "Again" and "Then" for Future Actions

再 (zài) goes before a verb and talks about something that has not happened yet: doing it again, doing a bit more, or doing it later, after something else. You already know it from 再见, "see you again".

"Again": doing it one more time

Subject + 再 + verb. 再 always comes before the verb, never at the end of the sentence as "again" does in English. If there is a word like 能 or 想, 再 goes after it and directly before the main verb.

Adding 一下 or doubling the verb (看看, 问问) makes the request sound lighter and more polite. 请再说一下 is the phrase to use when you didn't catch what someone said.

  1. 请再说一下。Qǐng zài shuō yíxià.Could you say that again, please?
  2. 我不认识这个字，你能再写一下吗？Wǒ bú rènshi zhège zì, nǐ néng zài xiě yíxià ma?I don't know this character. Could you write it again?
  3. 我明天再来。Wǒ míngtiān zài lái.I'll come again tomorrow.
  4. 我再问问老师。Wǒ zài wènwen lǎoshī.I'll ask the teacher again.

"More": a bit more, one more

With food, drink and shopping, 再 means "some more" or "another". It is often followed by 一点儿 or a number and measure word. Hosts in China say 你再吃一点儿吧 all the time.

  1. 你再吃一点儿吧。Nǐ zài chī yìdiǎnr ba.Have a bit more.
  2. 我还想再喝一点儿水。Wǒ hái xiǎng zài hē yìdiǎnr shuǐ.I'd like a little more water.
  3. 再买两个吧。Zài mǎi liǎng ge ba.Let's buy two more.

"Then" and "later": first this, then that

再 can also mean "then" or "only after that". The first action comes first in the sentence, often with 了, and 再 introduces the second one: 吃了饭再去 means "go after we've eaten".

On its own with a time word, 再 puts something off until later. 明天再说 ("let's talk about it tomorrow") is a polite way to stop a conversation or delay a decision.

  1. 吃了饭再去吧。Chī le fàn zài qù ba.Let's go after we've eaten.
  2. 你到了家再给我打电话。Nǐ dào le jiā zài gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.Call me when you get home.
  3. 我现在很忙，下午再给你打电话。Wǒ xiànzài hěn máng, xiàwǔ zài gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.I'm busy right now. I'll call you this afternoon.
  4. 我今天不去了，明天再去。Wǒ jīntiān bú qù le, míngtiān zài qù.I'm not going today. I'll go tomorrow.
  5. 好的，我们明天再说。Hǎo de, wǒmen míngtiān zài shuō.OK, let's talk about it tomorrow.

Negatives and questions

不要再 + verb + 了 tells someone to stop doing something, or not to do it again. 不想再 + verb says you don't want to do it again. 再也不 is stronger: "never again".

In questions, 再 stays in front of the verb: 你能不能再说一下? Note the tone change: 不 becomes bú before a fourth tone, as in 不要 búyào.

  1. 你不要再看手机了。Nǐ búyào zài kàn shǒujī le.Stop looking at your phone.
  2. 我不想再去那儿了。Wǒ bù xiǎng zài qù nàr le.I don't want to go there again.
  3. 她说她再也不想来了。Tā shuō tā zài yě bù xiǎng lái le.She said she never wants to come again.
  4. 要不要再买一个？Yào bu yào zài mǎi yí ge?Shall we buy another one?

The most common mistake: 再 is for the future

再 is only for repeats that haven't happened yet: plans, requests and suggestions. For something that already happened again, Chinese uses a different word, 又 (yòu), which you will meet at HSK 3. So "He came again yesterday" is 他昨天又来了, not 他昨天再来了.

A quick test: if the English sentence is about the past ("came again", "was late again"), don't use 再.

  1. 我不在家，你晚上再来吧。Wǒ bú zài jiā, nǐ wǎnshang zài lái ba.I'm not at home. Come back this evening.
