几 / 多少几 vs 多少: Asking "How Many"

Chinese has two ways to ask "how many". 几 (jǐ) is for small numbers and always needs a measure word. 多少 (duōshao) is for any number, large or small, and is also how you ask about prices and phone numbers.

几: small numbers

几 + measure word + noun.

Use 几 when you expect the answer to be a small number, usually under ten: people in a family, children, things you want to buy. 几 must always have a measure word after it, such as 个, 本 or 口. To answer, put the number where 几 was. There is no 吗 at the end, because 几 already makes it a question.

  1. 你家有几口人？Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?How many people are there in your family?
  2. 我家有三口人。Wǒ jiā yǒu sān kǒu rén.There are three people in my family.
  3. 你有几个孩子？Nǐ yǒu jǐ ge háizi?How many children do you have?
  4. 你要几个？Nǐ yào jǐ ge?How many do you want?

多少: any number

多少 + (measure word) + noun.

Use 多少 when the number could be large, or when you have no idea what it will be. With 多少 the measure word is optional: 多少学生 and 多少个学生 are both correct. 多少 is also the word for prices and for numbers such as phone numbers.

  1. 你们学校有多少学生？Nǐmen xuéxiào yǒu duōshao xuésheng?How many students are there at your school?
  2. 你认识多少汉字？Nǐ rènshi duōshao Hànzì?How many Chinese characters do you know?
  3. 你买了多少个苹果？Nǐ mǎi le duōshao ge píngguǒ?How many apples did you buy?
  4. 这个多少钱？Zhège duōshao qián?How much is this?
  5. 你的手机号是多少？Nǐ de shǒujī hào shì duōshao?What's your mobile number?

Time, dates and age use 几

Some everyday questions always use 几, because the answer is a small number: the hour (几点), the day of the week (星期几), the month and date (几月几号) and a child's age (几岁).

几岁 is for children. To ask an adult's age, say 你多大了 instead.

  1. 现在几点？Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?What time is it?
  2. 你几点下班？Nǐ jǐ diǎn xiàbān?What time do you finish work?
  3. 今天星期几？Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?What day is it today?
  4. 今天几月几号？Jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?What's the date today?
  5. 你女儿几岁了？Nǐ nǚ'ér jǐ suì le?How old is your daughter?
  6. 你多大了？Nǐ duō dà le?How old are you?

The most common mistake, and 几 meaning "a few"

The most common mistake is to leave out the measure word after 几. 你有几书 is wrong; say 你有几本书. If you are not sure which measure word to use, 多少 is the safer choice, because it works without one.

In a sentence that is not a question, 几 + measure word means "a few" or "several".

  1. 你有几本书？Nǐ yǒu jǐ běn shū?How many books do you have?
  2. 我有几个中国朋友。Wǒ yǒu jǐ ge Zhōngguó péngyou.I have a few Chinese friends.
  3. 我昨天买了几本书。Wǒ zuótiān mǎi le jǐ běn shū.I bought a few books yesterday.
