刚 vs 刚才刚 vs 刚才: Two Ways to Say "Just"

刚 (gāng) and 刚才 (gāngcái) both translate as "just", but they work differently. 刚 is an adverb that goes before the verb. 刚才 is a time word meaning "just now" or "a moment ago".

刚: "just", "only recently"

Subject + 刚 + verb.

刚 goes after the subject and directly before the verb. It means something happened only a short time ago, and "short" can be a few minutes or a few months, as the speaker feels it. You don't need 了 at the end: 我刚到 is a complete sentence.

  1. 我刚到。Wǒ gāng dào.I've just arrived.
  2. 他刚走。Tā gāng zǒu.He's just left.
  3. 我刚吃完饭。Wǒ gāng chīwán fàn.I've just finished eating.
  4. 他刚来中国两个月。Tā gāng lái Zhōngguó liǎng ge yuè.He's only been in China for two months.

刚才: "just now"

刚才 is a time word, like 今天 or 昨天. It can go at the start of the sentence or after the subject, and it only means a few minutes ago.

  1. 刚才有人给你打电话。Gāngcái yǒu rén gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.Someone called you just now.
  2. 你刚才去哪儿了？Nǐ gāngcái qù nǎr le?Where did you go just now?
  3. 刚才那个人是谁？Gāngcái nàge rén shì shéi?Who was that person just now?

Where they are different

Because 刚才 is a time word, it can do things 刚 can't. Only 刚才 can start a sentence: 刚我吃了饭 is wrong. Only 刚才 can come before a noun: 刚才的电话, not 刚的电话. Only 刚才 can go before a negative: 刚才我没有听见, never 刚没有.

刚 can do things 刚才 can't. It can go with a length of time (刚来两个月), and with 就 to say that one thing happened right after another: 我刚到家，就下雨了.

  1. 对不起，刚才我没有听见。Duìbuqǐ, gāngcái wǒ méiyǒu tīngjiàn.Sorry, I didn't hear you just now.
  2. 我刚到家，就下雨了。Wǒ gāng dào jiā, jiù xià yǔ le.I'd only just got home when it started to rain.
