跟……一样How to Say "the Same as" with 跟……一样

一样 (yíyàng) means "the same". With 跟 (gēn) or 和 (hé) in front of the second thing, A 跟 B 一样 says that A and B are the same. Add an adjective after it to say "as tall as", "as hot as" and so on.

A 跟 B 一样: "the same as"

A + 跟 + B + 一样.

跟 and 和 both work here and mean the same. To ask if two things are the same, add 吗 at the end. Before a noun, use 一样的: 一样的书包 is "the same school bag". With a verb, 跟……一样 means "like": 我跟你一样，也喜欢喝茶 is "Like you, I like tea too".

  1. 我的手机跟你的一样。Wǒ de shǒujī gēn nǐ de yíyàng.My phone is the same as yours.
  2. 你的书跟我的一样吗？Nǐ de shū gēn wǒ de yíyàng ma?Is your book the same as mine?
  3. 我和妹妹有一样的书包。Wǒ hé mèimei yǒu yíyàng de shūbāo.My little sister and I have the same school bag.
  4. 我跟你一样，也喜欢喝茶。Wǒ gēn nǐ yíyàng, yě xǐhuan hē chá.Like you, I like tea too.

A 跟 B 一样 + adjective: "as... as"

A + 跟 + B + 一样 + adjective.

The adjective goes after 一样, never before it. The most common mistake is to follow English word order and say 他跟我高一样; the right order is 他跟我一样高. To talk about how well someone does something, use a verb with 得 first.

  1. 他跟他爸爸一样高。Tā gēn tā bàba yíyàng gāo.He's as tall as his dad.
  2. 今天跟昨天一样热。Jīntiān gēn zuótiān yíyàng rè.Today is just as hot as yesterday.
  3. 这件衣服和那件一样贵。Zhè jiàn yīfu hé nà jiàn yíyàng guì.This top costs the same as that one.
  4. 他说汉语说得跟中国人一样好。Tā shuō Hànyǔ shuō de gēn Zhōngguórén yíyàng hǎo.He speaks Chinese as well as a Chinese person.

Negatives: 不一样 and 没有

To say two things are different, put 不 directly before 一样: A 跟 B 不一样. A common mistake is to put 不 before 跟 instead. Note the pinyin: 不一样 is bù yíyàng.

To say "not as tall as" or "not as expensive as", Chinese usually uses 没有 instead: A + 没有 + B + adjective.

  1. 这两个字不一样。Zhè liǎng ge zì bù yíyàng.These two characters are different.
  2. 中国人的名字跟我们的不一样。Zhōngguórén de míngzi gēn wǒmen de bù yíyàng.Chinese names are different from ours.
  3. 弟弟没有哥哥高。Dìdi méiyǒu gēge gāo.The younger brother isn't as tall as the older one.
