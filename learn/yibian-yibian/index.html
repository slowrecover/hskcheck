一边……一边How to Say "While" with 一边……一边

一边 (yìbiān) goes before two actions to show that one person is doing both at the same time, like eating while watching TV. It is the everyday way to say "while" or "as" in Chinese.

The pattern

Subject + 一边 + action 1, 一边 + action 2.

Put 一边 directly before each verb. Both actions are done by the same person and happen at the same time. The comma in the middle is optional. In English the order often changes: 我一边吃饭，一边看电视 is "I watch TV while I eat".

  1. 我一边吃饭，一边看电视。Wǒ yìbiān chī fàn, yìbiān kàn diànshì.I watch TV while I eat.
  2. 她一边听音乐，一边做作业。Tā yìbiān tīng yīnyuè, yìbiān zuò zuòyè.She listens to music while she does her homework.
  3. 我们一边喝咖啡，一边聊天。Wǒmen yìbiān hē kāfēi, yìbiān liáotiān.We chatted over coffee.
  4. 他一边上大学，一边工作。Tā yìbiān shàng dàxué, yìbiān gōngzuò.He works while he's at university.

The subject comes first

The most common mistake is to put the subject after 一边, as in 一边我吃饭. The subject goes before the first 一边, and you don't repeat it after the second one. Also remember the second 一边: 我一边吃饭，看电视 is not complete.

To tell someone not to do two things at once, put 别 or 不要 before the first 一边.

  1. 妈妈一边做饭，一边唱歌。Māma yìbiān zuò fàn, yìbiān chàng gē.Mum sings while she cooks.
  2. 别一边吃饭，一边看手机。Bié yìbiān chī fàn, yìbiān kàn shǒujī.Don't look at your phone while you're eating.
  3. 你不要一边开车，一边打电话。Nǐ búyào yìbiān kāichē, yìbiān dǎ diànhuà.Don't talk on the phone while you're driving.

The short form 边……边

In speech you will also hear 边……边, mostly with short, one-syllable verbs. It means the same thing.

  1. 我们边走边说吧。Wǒmen biān zǒu biān shuō ba.Let's talk as we walk.
  2. 孩子们边唱边跳。Háizimen biān chàng biān tiào.The children sang and danced.

Don't use it for adjectives

一边……一边 is only for actions. To say something has two qualities, such as big and sweet, use 又……又 instead: 又大又甜, not 一边大一边甜.

  1. 这个苹果又大又甜。Zhège píngguǒ yòu dà yòu tián.This apple is big and sweet.
  2. 这家饭馆的菜又便宜又好吃。Zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài yòu piányi yòu hǎochī.The food at this restaurant is cheap and tasty.
