最How to Use 最 (zuì): "The Most"

最 (zuì) means "the most". Put it in front of an adjective or a verb like 喜欢 and you get the English "-est" or "most": 最高 is "the tallest", 最喜欢 is "like best". It is a key word of HSK 2.

最 + adjective

Subject + 最 + adjective.

最 always comes directly before the adjective. You don't need 是, and you can't add 很 or 非常: 他最高 is right, 他最很高 is wrong. The group you are comparing within usually comes first, for example 我们家 (in our family) or 三个人里 (of the three people).

  1. 这个最便宜。Zhège zuì piányi.This one is the cheapest.
  2. 我们家我哥哥最高。Wǒmen jiā wǒ gēge zuì gāo.My older brother is the tallest in our family.
  3. 这家饭馆的菜最好吃。Zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài zuì hǎochī.The food at this restaurant is the best.

最 + 喜欢, 想, 爱

最 also goes before verbs of liking and wanting, such as 喜欢, 想 and 爱. 我最喜欢 means "I like best" or "my favourite is".

The most common mistake is to follow English word order and put 最 after the verb: 我喜欢最茶. 最 must come before the verb: 我最喜欢茶.

  1. 我最喜欢喝茶。Wǒ zuì xǐhuan hē chá.Tea is my favourite drink.
  2. 你最想去哪儿？Nǐ zuì xiǎng qù nǎr?Where would you most like to go?
  3. 我最爱吃米饭。Wǒ zuì ài chī mǐfàn.Rice is what I love eating most.

最 + adjective + 的 + noun

To say "the best friend" or "the biggest room", use 最 + adjective + 的 + noun. The 的 is needed: say 最好的朋友, not 最好朋友. 最 can never go straight before a noun.

If you want to use 是, end the sentence with 的: 他是最高的. 他是最高 on its own sounds unfinished; 他最高 is simpler.

  1. 她是我最好的朋友。Tā shì wǒ zuì hǎo de péngyou.She is my best friend.
  2. 这是我们学校最大的教室。Zhè shì wǒmen xuéxiào zuì dà de jiàoshì.This is the biggest classroom in our school.

Questions and negatives

To ask which one is the most, use 谁 (who) or 哪个 (which one) before 最. The answer keeps the same word order: 你们班谁最高？ 我们班王明最高。

最不 + verb or adjective means "the least": 我最不喜欢 is "what I like least".

  1. 你们班谁最高？Nǐmen bān shéi zuì gāo?Who is the tallest in your class?
  2. 这三个里，哪个最好？Zhè sān ge lǐ, nǎge zuì hǎo?Which of these three is the best?
  3. 我最不喜欢下雨天。Wǒ zuì bù xǐhuan xiàyǔtiān.Rainy days are what I like least.

Everyday words with 最

Three common words start with 最. 最近 means "recently" or "lately", 最后 means "in the end" or "last", and 最好 before a verb means "you'd better". Like other time words, 最近 and 最后 go at the start of the sentence or right after the subject.

  1. 我最近很忙。Wǒ zuìjìn hěn máng.I've been very busy lately.
  2. 最后我们坐出租车回家了。Zuìhòu wǒmen zuò chūzūchē huí jiā le.In the end we took a taxi home.
  3. 你最好去医院看看。Nǐ zuìhǎo qù yīyuàn kànkan.You'd better go and see a doctor.
