呢 · HSK 1 grammar
Name Date

AChoose 呢 or 吗

Write 呢 or 吗 in each gap. The English tells you what the sentence means.

  1. 我是学生，你？（呢 / 吗）I'm a student. What about you?
  2. 你喜欢吃米饭？（呢 / 吗）Do you like rice?
  3. 我的杯子？（呢 / 吗）Where's my cup?
  4. 他在看电视。（呢 / 吗）He's watching TV.
  5. 你今天忙？（呢 / 吗）Are you busy today?
  6. 你叫什么名字？（呢 / 吗）What's your name?
  7. 爸爸在打电话。（呢 / 吗）Dad is on the phone.
  8. 我今天不去，你？（呢 / 吗）I'm not going today. What about you?
  9. 他是你的朋友？（呢 / 吗）Is he your friend?
  10. 我们几点去？（呢 / 吗）So what time shall we go?

BPut the words in order

Write the words as one correct sentence.

  1. 在呢他们吃饭They're eating.
  2. 你呢妈妈Where's your mum?
  3. 做呢什么你在What are you doing?
  4. 正在呢学习我汉语I'm studying Chinese right now.
  5. 去哪儿我们明天呢So where shall we go tomorrow?

CFind and fix the mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Write the correct sentence.

  1. 你是老师呢？Are you a teacher?
  2. 你想去商店呢？Do you want to go to the shop?
  3. 我很忙，你吗？I'm very busy. And you?
  4. 你去哪儿吗？Where are you going?
  5. 我的电脑吗？Where's my computer?
