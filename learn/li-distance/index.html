离How to Use 离 to Say How Far Something Is

离 (lí) means "from" when you talk about distance: how far one place is from another. It is the word behind everyday sentences like "my home is close to school" and "is the station far from here?"

The pattern

A + 离 + B + 远 / 近.

Put the two places on either side of 离, and finish the sentence with 远 (far) or 近 (close). As with other adjectives, 远 and 近 normally take 很 in front of them, and you don't need 是. For the negative, put 不 before the adjective: 不远, 不近, or 不太远 (not very far).

  1. 我家离学校很近。Wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn.My home is very close to school.
  2. 机场离这儿很远。Jīchǎng lí zhèr hěn yuǎn.The airport is a long way from here.
  3. 医院离我家不远。Yīyuàn lí wǒ jiā bù yuǎn.The hospital isn't far from my home.
  4. 饭馆离这儿很近，我们走路去吧。Fànguǎn lí zhèr hěn jìn, wǒmen zǒulù qù ba.The restaurant is very close to here, so let's walk.

Asking how far

To ask if a place is far, add 吗 at the end, or use 远不远 (the 不 is said lightly, with a neutral tone). To ask how far, use 多远. In everyday speech people usually answer with the time it takes, such as 走路五分钟 (five minutes on foot), rather than with a distance.

  1. 你家离公司远吗？Nǐ jiā lí gōngsī yuǎn ma?Is your home far from your office?
  2. 车站离这儿远不远？Chēzhàn lí zhèr yuǎn bu yuǎn?Is the station far from here?
  3. 超市离你家多远？Chāoshì lí nǐ jiā duō yuǎn?How far is the supermarket from your home?
  4. 不太远，走路五分钟。Bú tài yuǎn, zǒulù wǔ fēnzhōng.Not very far, five minutes on foot.

离 or 从?

English uses "from" for both, but Chinese does not. 离 is for the gap between two places, with nothing moving. 从 is the starting point of a trip, and usually goes with 到: 从 A 到 B (from A to B). So 我家从学校很远 is wrong; say 我家离学校很远.

The other common mistake is English word order. In English "far" comes before "from school", but in Chinese 离 and the place come first and 远 or 近 goes at the end: 我家离学校很远, not 我家很远离学校.

  1. 我们学校离地铁站很近。Wǒmen xuéxiào lí dìtiězhàn hěn jìn.Our school is very close to the underground station.
  2. 从我家到学校要二十分钟。Cóng wǒ jiā dào xuéxiào yào èrshí fēnzhōng.It takes twenty minutes from my home to school.
  3. 他家离这儿很远，所以他每天开车上班。Tā jiā lí zhèr hěn yuǎn, suǒyǐ tā měi tiān kāichē shàngbān.He lives a long way from here, so he drives to work every day.

Counting down with 离…还有

离 can also measure time: how long it is until something happens. The pattern is 离 + event + 还有 + amount of time. Here too, use 离, not 从.

  1. 离上课还有十分钟。Lí shàngkè hái yǒu shí fēnzhōng.There are ten minutes until class starts.
  2. 离考试还有三天。Lí kǎoshì hái yǒu sān tiān.The exam is in three days.
  3. 现在离下班还有一个小时。Xiànzài lí xiàbān hái yǒu yí ge xiǎoshí.There's still an hour to go before we finish work.
