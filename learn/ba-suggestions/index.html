吧How to Use 吧 (ba)

吧 (ba) is a small word at the end of a sentence. It turns a sentence into a suggestion ("let's..."), makes a request sound softer, or shows that you are fairly sure of something and want the other person to agree. It is an HSK 1 grammar point and is said with a light, neutral tone.

"Let's...": 我们 + verb + 吧

Put 吧 at the end of a sentence with 我们 to suggest doing something together. 我们去吃饭 is a plain statement ("We're going to eat"); 我们去吃饭吧 is a suggestion ("Let's go and eat").

To suggest not doing something, put 不 before the verb, often with 了 to show a change of plan.

  1. 我们去吃饭吧。Wǒmen qù chī fàn ba.Let's go and eat.
  2. 我们坐出租车吧。Wǒmen zuò chūzūchē ba.Let's take a taxi.
  3. 八点了，我们起床吧。Bā diǎn le, wǒmen qǐchuáng ba.It's eight o'clock. Let's get up.
  4. 今天太冷了，我们不去了吧。Jīntiān tài lěng le, wǒmen bú qù le ba.It's too cold today. Let's not go.

Softer requests and advice: 你 + verb + 吧

With 你, 吧 turns an order into a friendly suggestion or invitation. 你坐 sounds like a command; 你坐吧 sounds like "Please, have a seat". It is very common when offering something or giving advice.

  1. 你坐吧。Nǐ zuò ba.Have a seat.
  2. 你休息一下吧。Nǐ xiūxi yíxià ba.Have a little rest.
  3. 下雨了，你开车去吧。Xià yǔ le, nǐ kāichē qù ba.It's raining. You'd better drive there.

Guesses: "..., right?"

吧 at the end of a question shows that you think you already know the answer and expect the other person to agree. It works like "..., right?", "..., aren't you?" or "I suppose..." in English.

In a statement, 吧 can also make a guess sound less certain, like "probably".

  1. 你是老师吧？Nǐ shì lǎoshī ba?You're a teacher, aren't you?
  2. 这是你的书吧？Zhè shì nǐ de shū ba?This is your book, right?
  3. 你不是中国人吧？Nǐ bú shì Zhōngguórén ba?You're not Chinese, are you?
  4. 明天会下雨吧。Míngtiān huì xià yǔ ba.It'll probably rain tomorrow.

好吧: "OK then"

好吧 means "OK" or "All right then". It agrees to something, often after a little hesitation, so it can sound less keen than a plain 好.

  1. 好吧，我们明天去。Hǎo ba, wǒmen míngtiān qù.All right then, we'll go tomorrow.

The most common mistake: 吧 vs 吗

Both 吗 and 吧 can end a yes/no question, but they are not the same. Use 吗 when you really don't know the answer. Use 吧 only when you think the answer is yes and are checking. Never put 吗 and 吧 together.

Also, don't add 吧 to a question that already has a question word such as 什么 or 哪儿. 你想吃什么吧？ is wrong; say 你想吃什么？. And remember that 吧 (ba) has a neutral tone, unlike 八 (bā) or 把 (bǎ).

  1. 你是学生吗？Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?Are you a student? (I have no idea.)
  2. 你是学生吧？Nǐ shì xuésheng ba?You're a student, aren't you? (I think so.)
  3. 你想吃什么？Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?What would you like to eat?
