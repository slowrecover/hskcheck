除了……以外How to Say "Except" and "Besides" with 除了……以外

除了……以外 (chúle... yǐwài) sets one thing apart from the rest. Whether it means "except" or "besides" depends on the word that comes next: 都 for "except", 还 or 也 for "besides" or "as well as".

With 都: "except"

除了 + A + 以外，(subject) + 都 + verb.

When the second part has 都 (all), A is the one exception: everyone or everything else is included, but not A. 以外 is optional, so 除了这个字 and 除了这个字以外 mean the same.

  1. 除了哥哥以外，我们都去了。Chúle gēge yǐwài, wǒmen dōu qù le.All of us went except my older brother.
  2. 除了星期天以外，他每天都工作。Chúle xīngqītiān yǐwài, tā měi tiān dōu gōngzuò.He works every day except Sunday.
  3. 除了这个字，别的我都认识。Chúle zhège zì, biéde wǒ dōu rènshi.I know all the others apart from this character.

With 还 or 也: "besides" and "as well as"

除了 + A + 以外，(subject) + 还 / 也 + verb.

When the second part has 还 or 也, A is included and something is added to it. 还 usually adds something else that the same person does. 也 is common when a different person does the same thing.

  1. 除了游泳以外，我还喜欢跑步。Chúle yóuyǒng yǐwài, wǒ hái xǐhuan pǎobù.Besides swimming, I also like running.
  2. 我们除了吃饭以外，还去看了电影。Wǒmen chúle chī fàn yǐwài, hái qù kàn le diànyǐng.As well as having dinner, we went to see a film.
  3. 除了我，妹妹也去过中国。Chúle wǒ, mèimei yě qù guo Zhōngguó.Besides me, my little sister has been to China too.

Word order and the most common mistake

The subject can come before 除了 or after the 除了 part: 我除了游泳以外 and 除了游泳以外，我 are both fine. 都, 还 and 也 always go after the subject and before the verb, so 除了他，都我们去了 is wrong.

The most common mistake is to leave out 都 or 还, or to use the wrong one. They change the meaning completely: 除了他，我们都去了 means he didn't go, while 除了他，我们也去了 means he went and so did we.

  1. 除了这个以外，别的都可以。Chúle zhège yǐwài, biéde dōu kěyǐ.Anything but this one is fine.

Negatives and questions

With 都不 or 都没有, the sentence means "nobody but" or "nothing but". To ask "what else?" or "who else?", use 还 with a question word.

  1. 除了他，大家都不知道这件事。Chúle tā, dàjiā dōu bù zhīdào zhè jiàn shì.Nobody knows about this except him.
  2. 除了面条以外，你还想吃什么？Chúle miàntiáo yǐwài, nǐ hái xiǎng chī shénme?What else would you like to eat besides noodles?
