更How to Say "Even More" with 更

更 (gèng) means "even more" or "-er". Put it before an adjective or a verb like 喜欢 to say that something goes one step further than something else.

更 + adjective

Subject + 更 + adjective.

更 always comes directly before the adjective. It usually means the first thing already has the quality and the second has even more of it: if today is cold, tomorrow will be even colder. You can use 更 without 比 when it is clear what you are comparing with.

  1. 今天很冷，明天会更冷。Jīntiān hěn lěng, míngtiān huì gèng lěng.It's cold today, and it'll be even colder tomorrow.
  2. 这件衣服很便宜，那件更便宜。Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn piányi, nà jiàn gèng piányi.This top is cheap, and that one is even cheaper.
  3. 多练习，你的汉语会更好。Duō liànxí, nǐ de Hànyǔ huì gèng hǎo.Practise more and your Chinese will get even better.

更 in 比 sentences

A + 比 + B + 更 + adjective.

A plain 比 sentence (他比我高) is already complete. Adding 更 makes it "even taller". The most common mistake is to use 很 instead: 他比我很高 is wrong. You will also hear 还 in the same place, with the same meaning: 他比我还高.

  1. 哥哥很高，弟弟比哥哥更高。Gēge hěn gāo, dìdi bǐ gēge gèng gāo.My older brother is tall, and my younger brother is even taller.
  2. 这个房间比那个房间更大。Zhège fángjiān bǐ nàge fángjiān gèng dà.This room is even bigger than that one.
  3. 他比我还高。Tā bǐ wǒ hái gāo.He's even taller than me.

更喜欢: "prefer"

更 also goes before verbs of liking, such as 喜欢 and 想. 我更喜欢 is the usual way to say "I prefer". To ask which one someone prefers, use 你更喜欢 with 哪个 or 还是.

  1. 我喜欢喝咖啡，但是我更喜欢喝茶。Wǒ xǐhuan hē kāfēi, dànshì wǒ gèng xǐhuan hē chá.I like coffee, but I like tea even more.
  2. 你更喜欢哪个颜色？Nǐ gèng xǐhuan nǎge yánsè?Which colour do you prefer?
  3. 我觉得这个办法更好。Wǒ juéde zhège bànfǎ gèng hǎo.I think this way is better.

更 vs 最

更 compares one thing with another: "more". 最 picks one out of a group: "the most". Both come before the adjective or verb, never after it, and neither is used together with 很: say 我更喜欢, not 我更很喜欢.

  1. 他很高，他哥哥更高，他爸爸最高。Tā hěn gāo, tā gēge gèng gāo, tā bàba zuì gāo.He's tall, his older brother is taller, and his dad is the tallest.
