会 / 能 / 可以会, 能 and 可以: Three Ways to Say "Can"

English has one word, "can", but Chinese has three. 会 (huì) is for skills you have learned, 能 (néng) is for being able to do something in the situation, and 可以 (kěyǐ) is for permission. All three go straight before the verb, and all three are HSK 1 words.

会: a skill you have learned

Subject + 会 + verb (+ object).

Use 会 for things you had to learn: speaking a language, driving, cooking, writing characters. 我会说中文 means you know how to speak Chinese. 不会 means you never learned how.

  1. 我会说中文。Wǒ huì shuō Zhōngwén.I can speak Chinese.
  2. 他不会开车。Tā bú huì kāichē.He can't drive.
  3. 你会做饭吗？Nǐ huì zuò fàn ma?Can you cook?
  4. 你会写这个字吗？Nǐ huì xiě zhège zì ma?Do you know how to write this character?

会: "will" and "is likely to"

会 has a second meaning: it says that something is likely to happen, a bit like "will" in English. You will hear it a lot when people talk about the weather or make guesses about the future. 不会 then means "won't" or "isn't going to".

  1. 明天会下雨吗？Míngtiān huì xià yǔ ma?Is it going to rain tomorrow?
  2. 晚上会很冷。Wǎnshang huì hěn lěng.It'll be cold tonight.
  3. 他今天不会来了。Tā jīntiān bú huì lái le.He won't be coming today.

能: able to, in this situation

能 is about whether you are able to do something at a given time: whether you are free, whether something works, or how much you can manage. It is less about learning and more about circumstances.

So someone who knows how to drive 会开车, but if they are too tired today, they 不能开车.

  1. 我明天要工作，不能去。Wǒ míngtiān yào gōngzuò, bù néng qù.I have to work tomorrow, so I can't go.
  2. 你明天能来吗？Nǐ míngtiān néng lái ma?Can you come tomorrow?
  3. 我的手机不能打电话了。Wǒ de shǒujī bù néng dǎ diànhuà le.My phone can't make calls any more.
  4. 他一天能看两本书。Tā yì tiān néng kàn liǎng běn shū.He can read two books in a day.

可以: permission

可以 is the usual word for asking for or giving permission: "may I...?", "you can...". 能 is also often used in questions like 我能……吗.

To say something is not allowed, 不能 is the most common choice. 不可以 is also correct but sounds firmer, like a rule or a parent talking to a child. When someone asks 可以吗, a short yes is 可以, and a short no is often 不行 (not OK).

  1. 我可以坐这儿吗？Wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma?Can I sit here?
  2. 老师，我可以回家吗？Lǎoshī, wǒ kěyǐ huí jiā ma?Can I go home, teacher?
  3. 我可以看一下你的书吗？Wǒ kěyǐ kàn yíxià nǐ de shū ma?Could I have a look at your book?
  4. 你可以明天再来。Nǐ kěyǐ míngtiān zài lái.You can come back tomorrow.
  5. 这儿不能吃东西。Zhèr bù néng chī dōngxi.You can't eat here.

Negatives, questions and the common mistake

Make all three negative with 不 before them: 不会 (bú huì), 不能, 不可以. Ask a yes/no question with 吗 at the end, or with verb-not-verb: 会不会, 能不能. To answer, just repeat the word: 会 or 不会, 能 or 不能.

The most common learner mistake is to use 可以 or 能 for a skill. For something you have learned, such as a language, 会 is the natural choice. 我可以说中文 sounds like "I'm allowed to speak Chinese" or "I can speak Chinese if you like", not a statement about what you have learned.

  1. 你会不会说中文？Nǐ huì bu huì shuō Zhōngwén?Can you speak Chinese?
  2. 你能不能来我家？Nǐ néng bu néng lái wǒ jiā?Can you come to my place?
