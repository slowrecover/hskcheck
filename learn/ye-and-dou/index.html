也 / 都也 and 都: Word Order with "Also" and "All"

也 (yě) means "also" or "too", and 都 (dōu) means "all" or "both". They follow the same word-order rule: after the subject, before the verb or adjective. They never go at the start or the end of the sentence.

也: also, too

Subject + 也 + verb or adjective.

In English, "too" can go at the end of the sentence. In Chinese, 也 always comes after the subject and before the verb or adjective, and also before words like 很, 不 and 想. This is true even when the "also" is about the object: "I like reading, and I also like films" is 我喜欢看书，也喜欢看电影, with 也 before the second 喜欢.

  1. 我也是学生。Wǒ yě shì xuésheng.I'm a student too.
  2. 我也很忙。Wǒ yě hěn máng.I'm busy too.
  3. 他也喜欢吃米饭。Tā yě xǐhuan chī mǐfàn.He likes rice too.
  4. 我喜欢看书，也喜欢看电影。Wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū, yě xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.I like reading, and I like watching films too.

都: all, both

Plural subject + 都 + verb or adjective.

都 looks back at the words in front of it and says that all of them are included: we, these books, Mum and I. With two people or things it means "both". It is very common after time words such as 天天 (every day).

  1. 我们都是老师。Wǒmen dōu shì lǎoshī.We're all teachers.
  2. 我和妈妈都喜欢看书。Wǒ hé māma dōu xǐhuan kàn shū.Mum and I both like reading.
  3. 这些书都很好看。Zhèxiē shū dōu hěn hǎokàn.These books are all really good.
  4. 我天天都七点起床。Wǒ tiāntiān dōu qī diǎn qǐchuáng.I get up at seven every day.

The most common mistake

Learners often put 也 or 都 where the English word goes. 也我是学生 and 我是学生也 are both wrong; say 我也是学生. In the same way, "all of us are going" is 我们都去, never 都我们去.

Because 都 only looks backwards, the things it covers must come before it. To say "I like both of these", move 这两个 to the front: 这两个我都喜欢. And when you use 也 and 都 together, 也 comes first: 也都.

  1. 这两个我都喜欢。Zhè liǎng ge wǒ dōu xǐhuan.I like both of these.
  2. 他们也都是中国人。Tāmen yě dōu shì Zhōngguó rén.They're all Chinese too.
  3. 我也是。Wǒ yě shì.Me too.

Negatives and questions

For negatives, 也 and 都 come before 不 or 没有. 也不 and 也没有 mean "not either". 都不 means "none of them", but 不都 means "not all of them", so the order changes the meaning.

To ask a question, just add 吗 at the end. The word order stays the same.

  1. 我也不知道。Wǒ yě bù zhīdào.I don't know either.
  2. 我昨天也没有去。Wǒ zuótiān yě méiyǒu qù.I didn't go yesterday either.
  3. 他们都不去。Tāmen dōu bú qù.None of them are going.
  4. 他们不都是学生。Tāmen bù dōu shì xuésheng.Not all of them are students.
  5. 你们都去吗？Nǐmen dōu qù ma?Are you all going?
  6. 你也想去吗？Nǐ yě xiǎng qù ma?Do you want to go too?
