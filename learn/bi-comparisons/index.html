比How to Compare Two Things with 比

比 (bǐ) is the everyday way to say that one thing is bigger, colder, cheaper or better than another. The pattern is short, but it has two rules that learners often break: no 很 before the adjective, and no 不 for the negative.

The pattern: A 比 B + adjective

A + 比 + B + adjective. Read it as "A, compared with B, is ...". The adjective comes at the end, and there is no word for "more": 我比他高 already means "I am taller than him".

A and B should be the same kind of thing. To compare my phone with yours, say 我的手机比你的新; the 的 at the end stands for 手机, so you don't need to repeat it.

  1. 我比他高。Wǒ bǐ tā gāo.I'm taller than him.
  2. 今天比昨天冷。Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng.Today is colder than yesterday.
  3. 这个比那个便宜。Zhège bǐ nàge piányi.This one is cheaper than that one.
  4. 我的手机比你的新。Wǒ de shǒujī bǐ nǐ de xīn.My phone is newer than yours.

No 很, but you can say how much

The most common mistake is to put 很 or 非常 before the adjective, as in 他比我很高. With 比 the adjective stands on its own: 他比我高.

To say how big the difference is, add something after the adjective: 一点儿 for "a bit", 多了 or 得多 for "much", or an exact amount such as 三岁. To say "even more", put 还 before the adjective.

  1. 他比我高一点儿。Tā bǐ wǒ gāo yìdiǎnr.He's a bit taller than me.
  2. 坐飞机比坐火车快多了。Zuò fēijī bǐ zuò huǒchē kuài duō le.Flying is much faster than going by train.
  3. 这个房间比那个大得多。Zhège fángjiān bǐ nàge dà de duō.This room is much bigger than that one.
  4. 哥哥比我大三岁。Gēge bǐ wǒ dà sān suì.My older brother is three years older than me.
  5. 今天比昨天还热。Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān hái rè.Today is even hotter than yesterday.

Negatives and questions

To say "A is not as ... as B", use 没有 instead of 比: A + 没有 + B + adjective. You can add 那么 before the adjective, meaning "as ... as that". Don't say 我比他不高.

For a yes-or-no question, add 吗 at the end of a 比 sentence.

  1. 我没有他高。Wǒ méiyǒu tā gāo.I'm not as tall as him.
  2. 今天没有昨天那么热。Jīntiān méiyǒu zuótiān nàme rè.Today isn't as hot as yesterday.
  3. 你比他大吗？Nǐ bǐ tā dà ma?Are you older than him?

Comparing how people do things

To compare actions, use a verb with 得 and put 比 either before the verb or after 得. Both word orders are correct and mean the same.

  1. 他跑得比我快。Tā pǎo de bǐ wǒ kuài.He runs faster than me.
  2. 他比我跑得快。Tā bǐ wǒ pǎo de kuài.He runs faster than me.
