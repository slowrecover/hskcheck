让How to Use 让 (ràng): Making or Letting Someone Do Something

让 (ràng) is the everyday word for getting someone else to do something. Depending on the context it means ask, tell, let or make, and it always comes before the person, with the action after them.

The pattern

A + 让 + B + verb.

A is the person (or thing) behind the action, B is the person who does it. The person comes straight after 让, and the action comes after the person. When the speaker is a teacher, a doctor or a parent, 让 usually means tell or ask someone to do something.

  1. 老师让我读这本书。Lǎoshī ràng wǒ dú zhè běn shū.The teacher asked me to read this book.
  2. 医生让他多休息。Yīshēng ràng tā duō xiūxi.The doctor told him to get plenty of rest.
  3. 朋友让我帮他找工作。Péngyou ràng wǒ bāng tā zhǎo gōngzuò.My friend asked me to help him find a job.
  4. 我让他明天给我打电话。Wǒ ràng tā míngtiān gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.I asked him to call me tomorrow.

Letting someone do something

让 also means let or allow. Without a subject, 让 + person + verb is a request or an offer: 让我看看 is the normal way to say let me have a look, and 让我来吧 offers to do something for someone.

  1. 让他进来吧。Ràng tā jìnlai ba.Let him come in.
  2. 让我看看。Ràng wǒ kànkan.Let me have a look.
  3. 让我想想。Ràng wǒ xiǎngxiang.Let me think.
  4. 让我来吧。Ràng wǒ lái ba.Let me do it.

Making someone feel something

After the person, 让 can be followed by an adjective or a feeling instead of an action. Then it means make: something makes someone happy, tired or not want to do something. The first part is often a thing or an event, not a person.

  1. 这件事让我很高兴。Zhè jiàn shì ràng wǒ hěn gāoxìng.This made me very happy.
  2. 考试让我很累。Kǎoshì ràng wǒ hěn lèi.Exams make me really tired.
  3. 这么冷的天气让我不想出门。Zhème lěng de tiānqì ràng wǒ bù xiǎng chūmén.Cold weather like this makes me want to stay in.

Negatives and questions

To say someone won't let you do something, put 不 before 让: 不让. For the past, use 没有让. If someone told you not to do something, the negative goes after the person instead, with 不要 or 别: 老师让我们不要说话 means the teacher told us not to talk.

For a question, add 吗 at the end, or use 让不让.

  1. 爸爸不让我看电视。Bàba bú ràng wǒ kàn diànshì.Dad won't let me watch TV.
  2. 妈妈没有让我去。Māma méiyǒu ràng wǒ qù.Mum didn't let me go.
  3. 老师让我们不要说话。Lǎoshī ràng wǒmen búyào shuōhuà.The teacher told us not to talk.
  4. 你妈妈让你去吗？Nǐ māma ràng nǐ qù ma?Is your mum letting you go?
  5. 你妈妈让不让你去？Nǐ māma ràng bu ràng nǐ qù?Is your mum going to let you go or not?

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is to translate make with 做. 做 means to do or make a thing, such as food or homework. To make someone feel or do something, use 让: 他让我很高兴, not 他做我很高兴.

Also, 了 does not go straight after 让. Put it after the second verb or at the end of the sentence.

  1. 他让我很高兴。Tā ràng wǒ hěn gāoxìng.He made me very happy.
  2. 他让我们等了一个小时。Tā ràng wǒmen děng le yí ge xiǎoshí.He kept us waiting for an hour.
