才 就才 vs 就: "Only Then" or "Already"

才 (cái) and 就 (jiù) both go before the verb and show how the speaker feels about the timing. 就 says something happened early, quickly or easily; 才 says it happened late, slowly or with difficulty.

就 is early, 才 is late

Time + 就 + verb + 了. Time + 才 + verb.

Both words come after the time word and straight before the verb. 就 says something happened earlier than expected. 才 says it happened later than expected, like "not until" in English. For something that has already happened, a 就 sentence usually ends in 了, but a 才 sentence doesn't.

  1. 他六点就起床了。Tā liù diǎn jiù qǐchuáng le.He was up as early as six.
  2. 他十点才起床。Tā shí diǎn cái qǐchuáng.He didn't get up until ten.
  3. 电影七点开始，他七点半才到。Diànyǐng qī diǎn kāishǐ, tā qī diǎn bàn cái dào.The film started at seven, but he didn't arrive until half past.

Quick or slow, young or old

The same idea works with lengths of time and with ages. With 就, it took less time than expected or happened at a young age. With 才, it took a long time or came late.

  1. 我半个小时就做完了。Wǒ bàn ge xiǎoshí jiù zuòwán le.I finished it in just half an hour.
  2. 他三个小时才做完。Tā sān ge xiǎoshí cái zuòwán.It took him three hours to finish.
  3. 他二十二岁就结婚了。Tā èrshí'èr suì jiù jiéhūn le.He was already married at twenty-two.
  4. 她三十八岁才结婚。Tā sānshíbā suì cái jiéhūn.She didn't get married until she was thirty-eight.

才 meaning "only"

Straight before a number, 才 means "only": the speaker thinks the number is small. It is often used with ages, clock times and prices.

  1. 他才八岁。Tā cái bā suì.He's only eight.
  2. 现在才六点，你再睡一会儿吧。Xiànzài cái liù diǎn, nǐ zài shuì yíhuìr ba.It's only six. Go back to sleep for a bit.

After a condition

After a condition, 就 means "then": if the first thing happens, the second follows. 才 means "only then": the second thing happens only if the first one does.

  1. 你去，我就去。Nǐ qù, wǒ jiù qù.If you go, I'll go.
  2. 你去，我才去。Nǐ qù, wǒ cái qù.I'll only go if you go.

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is putting 了 at the end of a 才 sentence about the past. Say 他十点才来, not 他十点才来了.

Neither word can go at the end of the sentence. When they mean "late" or "early", put them after the subject and the time word, straight before the verb.

  1. 我昨天晚上十二点才睡觉。Wǒ zuótiān wǎnshang shí'èr diǎn cái shuìjiào.I didn't go to bed until twelve last night.
  2. 我们八点就到了。Wǒmen bā diǎn jiù dào le.We got there as early as eight.
