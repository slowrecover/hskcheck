几月几号Dates: 年, 月, 号 and Days of the Week

Chinese dates go from the biggest unit to the smallest: year (年 nián), then month (月 yuè), then day (号 hào), then the day of the week (星期 xīngqī). So 17 October 2026 is 2026年10月17号.

Biggest first: year, month, day

Year + 年, month + 月, day + 号. The order never changes, so a learner's most common mistake is to follow English and say 十七号十月. The correct order is 十月十七号.

Years are read one digit at a time: 2026年 is èr líng èr liù nián, not "two thousand and twenty-six". Months are just the numbers 一 to 十二 plus 月, so 一月 is January and 十二月 is December. In speech the day is 号; in writing, on forms and signs, you will also see 日 (rì).

  1. 今天是2026年10月17号。Jīntiān shì èr líng èr liù nián shí yuè shíqī hào.Today is 17 October 2026.
  2. 现在是2026年。Xiànzài shì èr líng èr liù nián.It's 2026 now.
  3. 他去年九月去中国了。Tā qùnián jiǔ yuè qù Zhōngguó le.He went to China last September.

二月 is February, 两个月 is two months

A month name uses the plain number with no measure word: 二月 is February. To count how many months, add 个 and use 两 for two: 两个月 is "two months". Mixing these up is a very common mistake, so never say 两月.

  1. 现在是二月，天很冷。Xiànzài shì èr yuè, tiān hěn lěng.It's February now, and it's very cold.
  2. 他在中国工作了两个月。Tā zài Zhōngguó gōngzuò le liǎng ge yuè.He worked in China for two months.
  3. 你几月去中国？Nǐ jǐ yuè qù Zhōngguó?Which month are you going to China?

Days of the week with 星期

Monday to Saturday are 星期 plus a number: 星期一 is Monday, 星期六 is Saturday. Sunday is different: 星期天 in speech or 星期日 in writing, never 星期七. To ask which day it is, put 几 where the number goes: 星期几.

For "next" and "last", use 下个 and 上个: 下个星期三 is next Wednesday, 上个月 is last month. For years, use 去年, 今年 and 明年 instead.

  1. 今天星期几？Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?What day is it today?
  2. 今天星期六。Jīntiān xīngqīliù.Today is Saturday.
  3. 我星期天不上课。Wǒ xīngqītiān bú shàngkè.I don't have classes on Sunday.
  4. 下个星期三我去医院。Xià ge xīngqīsān wǒ qù yīyuàn.I'm going to the hospital next Wednesday.

Asking the date, negatives and word order

To ask the date, put 几 in place of the numbers: 几月几号. You don't need 是 when you give the date, but you do need it in a negative: 今天不是星期六, not 今天不星期六.

When you say when something happens, the date goes before the verb, either before or after the subject: 我们十月二十号回家 or 十月二十号我们回家. Putting it at the end, as in English, is wrong.

  1. 今天几月几号？Jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?What's the date today?
  2. 明天不是十号，是十一号。Míngtiān bú shì shí hào, shì shíyī hào.Tomorrow isn't the 10th, it's the 11th.
  3. 我们十月二十号回家。Wǒmen shí yuè èrshí hào huí jiā.We're going home on 20 October.
