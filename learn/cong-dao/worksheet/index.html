从……到 · HSK 2 grammar
Name Date

AChoose 从 or 到

Write 从 or 到 in each gap. The English tells you what the sentence means.

  1. 我从早上八点工作下午四点。（从 / 到）I work from eight in the morning until four in the afternoon.
  2. 从我家学校要十分钟。（从 / 到）It takes ten minutes to get from my home to school.
  3. 他是哪儿来的？（从 / 到）Where is he from?
  4. 我们的课从八点十点。（从 / 到）Our class is from eight to ten.
  5. 我星期一到星期五都在学校吃午饭。（从 / 到）From Monday to Friday I have lunch at school.
  6. 我们从这儿走火车站吧。（从 / 到）Let's walk from here to the train station.
  7. 今天我要工作晚上十点。（从 / 到）Today I have to work until ten in the evening.
  8. 明天开始，我每天早上跑步。（从 / 到）Starting tomorrow, I'm going running every morning.

BPut the words in order

Write the words as one correct sentence.

  1. 到从医院要我家半个小时It takes half an hour to get from my home to the hospital.
  2. 到他几点从几点工作What time does he start and finish work?
  3. 从到远吗你家学校Is it far from your home to school?
  4. 爸爸从早到晚都在工作Dad is working from morning till night.
  5. 你们从来是哪个国家的Which country are you from? (talking to more than one person)
  6. 不在家我从下午两点到四点I'm not at home from two to four in the afternoon.

CFind and fix the mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Write the correct sentence.

  1. 他工作从早上九点到下午五点。He works from nine in the morning to five in the afternoon.
  2. 我在中国从一月到三月。I'm in China from January to March.
  3. 我家从学校很远。My home is a long way from school.
  4. 从这儿火车站要五分钟。It takes five minutes to get from here to the train station.
  5. 他是中国从来的。He came from China.
  6. 我们看电视从八点到十点。We watch TV from eight to ten.
