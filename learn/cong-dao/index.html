从……到How to Say "From... To" with 从……到

从……到…… (cóng... dào...) means "from... to" or "from... until". You use it for times (from nine to five), days and months, and places (from my home to school). It is one of the key grammar points of HSK 2.

The pattern: from A to B

从 + starting point + 到 + end point.

从 (cóng) means "from" and 到 (dào) means "to" or "until". The two points can be times, days, months or places. With times, the whole 从……到…… phrase goes before the verb, like other time words in Chinese. It can also stand on its own after the subject, as in 电影从七点到九点 (the film is on from seven to nine).

  1. 我从九点到五点工作。Wǒ cóng jiǔ diǎn dào wǔ diǎn gōngzuò.I work from nine to five.
  2. 我们从星期一到星期五上课。Wǒmen cóng xīngqīyī dào xīngqīwǔ shàngkè.We have classes from Monday to Friday.
  3. 电影从七点到九点。Diànyǐng cóng qī diǎn dào jiǔ diǎn.The film is on from seven to nine.

From one place to another

With places, 从 A 到 B usually starts the sentence, and what follows says how far it is (远, 不远) or how long it takes (要 + time). Put how you travel, such as 坐地铁 or 坐出租车, before 要.

Don't mix it up with 离 (lí). 离 says how far one place is from another and needs no 从 or 到: 我家离学校很远. A common mistake is 我家从学校很远, which uses 从 where 离 is needed.

  1. 从我家到学校要二十分钟。Cóng wǒ jiā dào xuéxiào yào èrshí fēnzhōng.It takes twenty minutes to get from my home to school.
  2. 从这儿到那儿坐地铁要半个小时。Cóng zhèr dào nàr zuò dìtiě yào bàn ge xiǎoshí.It's half an hour from here to there by metro.
  3. 从医院到我家不远。Cóng yīyuàn dào wǒ jiā bù yuǎn.It isn't far from the hospital to my home.
  4. 我家离学校很远。Wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn yuǎn.My home is a long way from school.

Before the verb, not after

In English, "from nine to five" comes at the end: I work from nine to five. In Chinese, 从……到…… comes before the verb: 我从九点到五点工作, not 我工作从九点到五点. This is the most common mistake with the pattern.

The phrase can come after the subject or at the very start of the sentence. 都 is often added after it to stress "the whole time", as in the common phrase 从早到晚 (from morning till night).

  1. 我从下午两点到四点有课。Wǒ cóng xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn dào sì diǎn yǒu kè.I have a class from two to four in the afternoon.
  2. 这个星期我从星期一到星期四都很忙。Zhège xīngqī wǒ cóng xīngqīyī dào xīngqīsì dōu hěn máng.This week I'm busy every day from Monday to Thursday.
  3. 妈妈从早到晚都很忙。Māma cóng zǎo dào wǎn dōu hěn máng.Mum is busy from morning till night.

Questions and negatives

To ask a question, put a question word inside the pattern (从几点到几点) or add 吗 at the end. To ask how long a journey takes, use 要多长时间.

To say that something does not happen during that time, put 不 after the 从……到…… phrase, right before the verb.

  1. 你从几点到几点上课？Nǐ cóng jǐ diǎn dào jǐ diǎn shàngkè?What time do your classes start and finish?
  2. 从这儿到火车站远吗？Cóng zhèr dào huǒchēzhàn yuǎn ma?Is it far from here to the train station?
  3. 从这儿到机场坐出租车要多长时间？Cóng zhèr dào jīchǎng zuò chūzūchē yào duō cháng shíjiān?How long does it take to get from here to the airport by taxi?
  4. 我从八点到九点不在家。Wǒ cóng bā diǎn dào jiǔ diǎn bú zài jiā.I'm not at home from eight to nine.

从 and 到 on their own

从 can be used without 到: 从哪儿来 (come from where), 从明天开始 (starting from tomorrow). 到 can also follow a verb to mean "to" a place or "until" a time: 走到学校 (walk to school), 工作到十点 (work until ten).

This verb + 到 is the one case where 到 comes after the verb, and it can be combined with 从: 我从九点工作到五点 means the same as 我从九点到五点工作. But 从 itself never comes after the verb.

  1. 你是从哪儿来的？Nǐ shì cóng nǎr lái de?Where are you from?
  2. 从明天开始，我不喝咖啡了。Cóng míngtiān kāishǐ, wǒ bù hē kāfēi le.Starting tomorrow, I'm not drinking coffee any more.
  3. 我每天从家里走到学校。Wǒ měi tiān cóng jiā li zǒudào xuéxiào.I walk from home to school every day.
  4. 昨天我工作到晚上十点。Zuótiān wǒ gōngzuò dào wǎnshang shí diǎn.Yesterday I worked until ten in the evening.
