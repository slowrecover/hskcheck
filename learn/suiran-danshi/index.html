虽然……但是How to Say "Although" with 虽然……但是

虽然 (suīrán) means "although" and 但是 (dànshì) means "but". In Chinese they normally work as a pair: 虽然 introduces a fact, and 但是 introduces something you might not expect because of it.

The pattern

虽然 + fact, 但是 + unexpected result.

In English you say either "although" or "but", never both in one sentence. In Chinese, using both is normal and natural: 虽然 starts the first part of the sentence, and 但是 starts the second part.

  1. 虽然这件衣服很贵，但是很漂亮。Suīrán zhè jiàn yīfu hěn guì, dànshì hěn piàoliang.Although this piece of clothing is expensive, it's very pretty.
  2. 虽然汉语不好学，但是很有意思。Suīrán Hànyǔ bù hǎo xué, dànshì hěn yǒu yìsi.Although Chinese isn't easy to learn, it's very interesting.
  3. 虽然他是中国人，但是他不会做中国菜。Suīrán tā shì Zhōngguó rén, dànshì tā bú huì zuò Zhōngguó cài.Although he's Chinese, he can't cook Chinese food.

Where 虽然 and 但是 go

虽然 can come before or after the subject of the first part: 虽然他很忙 and 他虽然很忙 are both correct. When both parts are about the same person, you don't need to say the subject again after 但是.

但是 is different: it always comes at the very start of the second part, before the subject. Negatives with 不 and 没有 work as usual inside each part.

  1. 虽然他很忙，但是每天都学习汉语。Suīrán tā hěn máng, dànshì měi tiān dōu xuéxí Hànyǔ.Although he's busy, he studies Chinese every day.
  2. 他虽然很忙，但是每天都学习汉语。Tā suīrán hěn máng, dànshì měi tiān dōu xuéxí Hànyǔ.Although he's busy, he studies Chinese every day.
  3. 我虽然很累，但是不想睡觉。Wǒ suīrán hěn lèi, dànshì bù xiǎng shuìjiào.Although I'm tired, I don't want to go to sleep.

但是 on its own, and adding 还是

但是 can be used without 虽然, just like "but" in English. 虽然, on the other hand, is rarely left on its own: the second part normally has 但是, 还是 or both.

还是 here means "still" or "anyway". It goes after the subject of the second part, before the verb.

  1. 这个饭馆不大，但是菜很好吃。Zhège fànguǎn bú dà, dànshì cài hěn hǎochī.This restaurant isn't big, but the food is very good.
  2. 虽然天气不好，但是我们还是去了。Suīrán tiānqì bù hǎo, dànshì wǒmen háishi qù le.Although the weather wasn't good, we still went.
  3. 虽然医生说他要多休息，他还是去上班了。Suīrán yīshēng shuō tā yào duō xiūxi, tā háishi qù shàngbān le.Although the doctor told him to rest more, he went to work anyway.

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is to put 但是 after the subject, as in 虽然他很忙，他但是每天都来. 但是 must come first: 虽然他很忙，但是他每天都来.

Another mistake is mixing up the two pairs 虽然……但是 (although… but) and 因为……所以 (because… so). 虽然 goes with 但是, and 因为 goes with 所以. 虽然……所以 and 因为……但是 are both wrong.

  1. 因为太贵了，所以我没有买。Yīnwèi tài guì le, suǒyǐ wǒ méiyǒu mǎi.Because it was too expensive, I didn't buy it.
  2. 虽然很贵，但是我买了。Suīrán hěn guì, dànshì wǒ mǎi le.Although it was expensive, I bought it.
  3. 虽然他很忙，但是他每天都来。Suīrán tā hěn máng, dànshì tā měi tiān dōu lái.Although he's busy, he comes every day.
