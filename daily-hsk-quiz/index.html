    About the daily quiz

    What is in the quiz?

    Ten questions on the HSK 3.0 words of your level: the meaning of a word, the word for a meaning, the right pinyin, a word you hear, and at HSK 1–3 a gap to fill in a sentence from the grammar pages. You see the answer after every question.

    Why is it the same for everyone?

    The questions are picked from the date, so everyone at the same level gets the same quiz on the same day, and a new one at midnight. Compare your score with a classmate, or do it together with your teacher.

    How does the streak work?

    Finish a quiz at any level and the day counts. Your streak is the number of days in a row you have finished one, and the week shows your score at the level you have chosen. It is all kept in this browser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere.

    Want more practice?

    Take a longer HSK practice test of 20 questions as often as you like, or learn new words every day with the flashcards.

    Words. HSK 3.0 (New HSK) vocabulary, 2025 syllabus, the same lists as the word list pages and the practice test. Listening questions use your device's Chinese voice; without one, you choose the word for its meaning instead.
