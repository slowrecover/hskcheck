About HSK 1

HSK 1 is the first of the nine HSK 3.0 levels. Its 300 words cover numbers, family, time, food, places and the most common verbs, enough for short everyday exchanges.

Every later level builds on this list: HSK 2 adds 200 words for a total of 500, and HSK 3 adds 500 more for a total of 1,000.

A few entries are easy to miss because the syllabus lists words, not characters. 杯子 (cup) is on the list, but the measure word 杯 is not, so 一杯茶 is above HSK 1.

Source. Words and their order follow the HSK 3.0 Level 1 list in the 2025 exam syllabus, cross-checked against two independently published copies of the list.
