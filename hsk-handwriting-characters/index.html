      Reading and writing in HSK 3.0

      Why are there two character lists?

      The HSK 3.0 syllabus grades characters twice. One list sets the characters you must recognise at each level, 3,088 in total. A second, much shorter list sets the characters you must be able to write by hand: 1,200 in total. Every character on the writing list is also on the reading list, at the same level or earlier.

      How many characters do I need to write at each level?

      The counts build up: each level adds new characters to the ones before it. Levels 1 and 2 share a single list of 100.

      LevelNew to writeTotal to write
      HSK 1–2100100
      HSK 3150250
      HSK 4150400
      HSK 5150550
      HSK 6150700
      HSK 7–95001,200

      For comparison, by the end of HSK 3 you need to read 655 characters, and by the end of HSK 6, 1,940.

      Does this mean I can skip writing the other characters?

      For the exam, yes: only the characters on the writing list are expected by hand. Many learners still practise writing the most common reading characters, because writing a character a few times helps you remember it. Use the lists below to decide what to practise first.

      What colours does the checker use?

      Green characters are on the writing list at or below your level. Blue characters are on the writing list for a higher level. Grey characters only need to be read, and red characters are not on any HSK 3.0 list.
