About these worksheets

The YCT word lists are cumulative, so each level repeats the words of the levels below. These sheets use only the words that are new at YCT 2, split into sets of about 20, in the order of the word list.

Exercise A tests meaning and exercise B tests pinyin. Grammar words such as particles only appear in exercise B. Within each exercise no two words share a meaning or a pinyin, so there is always exactly one right answer.

Turn off “Pinyin in exercise A” to make the meaning exercise a pure reading test. For writing practice, print the YCT 2 character worksheets.
