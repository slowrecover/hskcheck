YCT vs HSK: Which HSK Level Is Each YCT Level?

Most YCT words are HSK 1–3 words: 92% of YCT 3 and 80% of YCT 4. But YCT 4, the top level, covers only 484 of the 997 different words in HSK 1–3. In vocabulary, YCT 1–2 are mostly HSK 1 words, YCT 3 reaches into HSK 2 and 3, and YCT 4 spreads across HSK 1–3 without covering any of them completely.

What this means

A student who passes YCT 4 knows a good part of the HSK 1–3 vocabulary, but not all of it. Moving on to the HSK, the quickest path is to learn the HSK 1–3 words that are not in the YCT list, rather than starting HSK 1 from scratch.

The YCT is written for children, so its list includes words the HSK leaves for later, such as animals, sports and single-character colours like 红 and 黄, and a few everyday phrases that the HSK does not list as separate words. Those are shown below.

This page compares word lists only. The YCT and the HSK are different exams with their own formats, and there is no official equivalence between their levels.
