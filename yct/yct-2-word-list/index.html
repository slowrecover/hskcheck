About YCT 2

YCT 2 has 150 words: the 80 words of YCT 1 plus 70 new ones. The new words add school things such as 铅笔 and 书包, colours, the weather, polite phrases such as 对不起 and 没关系, and question words like 怎么 and 多少.

This level also brings in the first grammar words beyond the basics: 了, 呢, 也 and 比 for comparisons.

Source. Words and pinyin follow the official YCT Level 2 syllabus (新中小学生汉语考试 YCT 大纲), cross-checked against an independently published copy of the list. The list is shown in alphabetical order of pinyin.

Meanings. The syllabus gives no translations. Meanings here give the most common senses only.
