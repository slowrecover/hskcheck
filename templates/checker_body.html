    About HSK 3.0 and this checker

    What is HSK 3.0?

    HSK 3.0, often called the New HSK, is the updated version of the Chinese proficiency test. It has nine levels instead of six, and a new vocabulary syllabus that sets how many words a learner should know at each level.

    How many words are in each level?

    Word targets in the 2025 syllabus are cumulative: each level includes all the words from the levels below it.

    Who is it for?

    Teachers use it to check that a reading passage, worksheet or exam question stays within their students' level. Learners use it to tell whether an article or graded reader is worth starting now, and which words to learn first.

    Why is 杯 in 一杯茶 marked, when 杯子 is Level 1?

    The syllabus lists words, not characters. 杯子 (cup) is a Level 1 word, but 杯 on its own, used as a measure word, is not in the Level 1–5 lists. The checker follows the lists exactly, so it marks it.

    Is my text stored anywhere?

    No. The check runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server.

    Word lists. New HSK (HSK 3.0) vocabulary, 2025 syllabus: Level 1 (300 words), Level 2 (200 new), Level 3 (500 new), Level 4 (1,000 new), Level 5 (1,600 new). Levels 6–9 will be added.

    How words are split. The checker finds the split with the fewest unknown characters, using only these lists. Names and rare words show as “not in HSK 1–5”.

    Reading guide. Reading research (Hu & Nation, 2000) suggests learners read comfortably when about 98% of running words are known. 95–98% is readable with help; below 95% is hard going.
