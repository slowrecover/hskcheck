How it works

New words and reviews

Each day you get a few new words from your level, in syllabus order, plus the words you are due to review. Say the meaning to yourself, then tap Show answer.

Got it, or again

If you knew it, the word comes back later: after 1 day, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. If not, it comes back at the end of today's session and again tomorrow. Words you already know can be skipped with I know this word.

Where is my progress kept?

In this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or using another phone or computer, starts you from the beginning.
