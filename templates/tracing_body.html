About character tracing

How is a stroke checked?

Each stroke has a centre line from where the brush starts to where it lifts. Your stroke is accepted when it starts near the start, ends near the end and follows the shape in between, so neat handwriting is not needed, but the direction is: horizontal strokes go left to right and vertical strokes top to bottom. A stroke drawn the wrong way round, or one that belongs later in the order, is not accepted, and the page tells you why.

What do Hint and Stroke order do?

Hint draws the next stroke for you slowly, from its start, so you can see where it goes and which way; then you write it yourself. After two misses on the same stroke the hint appears by itself. Stroke order plays the whole character. On a keyboard, press H for a hint, P to play the stroke order, R to start the character again and Enter for the next character.

Which characters are in each set?

The same characters as the character worksheets: every character used in the words of a level, in the order it first appears in the word list, without the characters of lower levels, in sets of 10. Each one is shown with the first word it appears in. Choose "Any characters" to write your own, such as your name or the new words of a lesson.

Where is my score kept?

A character counts when you write it without a mistake and without a hint. Your best for each set is kept in this browser on this device, and nothing is sent anywhere. To write on paper as well, print the same characters from the worksheet maker.
