About pinyin and tone practice

How do I type the tones?

Put a number after each syllable: 1 for the first tone (mā), 2 for the second (má), 3 for the third (mǎ), 4 for the fourth (mà), and 5 or nothing for the neutral tone, so 你好 is ni3hao3 and 朋友 is peng2you5 or peng2you. Tone marks work too, if your keyboard has them. Type ü as v (女 is nv3) or u:. Spaces, apostrophes and capital letters do not matter. On a phone, the buttons under the box add the numbers and ü.

Which pinyin counts as right?

The readings of the HSK 3.0 word list, the same as on the word list pages. Pinyin writes each syllable with its own tone, so 你好 is nǐ hǎo even though it is said ní hǎo. The one exception is 一 and 不: the word lists write their changed tones (一起 yìqǐ, 不客气 bú kèqi), and the original tones (yīqǐ, bù kèqi) are accepted too.

What do the tone numbers mean?

In "Choose the tones", 3-3 means two third tones, like 你好 nǐ hǎo, and 5 is the neutral tone, like the second syllable of 朋友 péngyou. The answer is the tones as written. When you hear a word, words whose tones change in speech (a third tone before a third tone, 一 and 不) are left out, so what you hear is what is written. Read more about tone changes.

Where is my score kept?

Your best score for each level and mode is kept in this browser on this device, and nothing is sent anywhere. Words you miss can go straight into the flashcards. To train your ear with whole sentences, try the listening practice.
