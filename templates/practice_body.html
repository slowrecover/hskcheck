About this practice test

What is in the test?

Twenty questions drawn at random from the words of the level you choose, so every test is different. You choose the meaning of a word, the word for a meaning, and the right pinyin; you hear a word and pick it out; and at HSK 1–3 you fill the gap in a sentence from the grammar pages.

Is this the HSK exam?

No. It is a practice test for the vocabulary of each level, made from the HSK 3.0 word lists. The exam itself has its own listening, reading and writing papers. A good score here means you know the words that the exam expects at your level.

Where is my score kept?

Your best score for each level is kept in this browser on this device. Your answers are not sent anywhere. Words you miss can go straight into the flashcards, which bring them back for review.

Words. HSK 3.0 (New HSK) vocabulary, 2025 syllabus, the same lists as the word list pages. Listening questions use your device's Chinese voice and appear only when it has one.
