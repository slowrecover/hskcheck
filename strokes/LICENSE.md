# Stroke data licence

The files in this folder are generated by `tools/make_stroke_data.py` (and, for `m/`,
`tools/make_median_data.py`) from [Make Me a Hanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi) (commit bddc96d).

- **Stroke outlines** (`"s"` in every file) come from `graphics.txt`, which is derived from the
  fonts Arphic PL KaitiM GB and Arphic PL UKai. They are redistributed under the
  **Arphic Public License**: see `ARPHICPL.TXT`. The stroke centre lines in `m/` (the `medians` of
  `graphics.txt`, used by the character tracing page) come from the same file under the same licence.
- **Pinyin** (`"p"` in every file) comes from `dictionary.txt`, derived from Unihan and CJKlib.
  It is redistributed under the **GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later**: see `LGPL.txt`.

The only changes are format changes: whitespace in the SVG paths is compacted, at most three
readings are kept, median points are rounded and flipped to screen direction, and the characters are regrouped into level files and shards.
