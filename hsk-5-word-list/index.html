About HSK 5

HSK 5 adds 1,600 words to the 2,000 of HSK 1–4, for 3,600 in total. It is the level where learners read the news, follow longer talks and discuss work, society and ideas, so abstract words such as 趋势 (trend), 措施 (measure) and 本质 (essence) become common.

Everyday modern life is well represented too: 二维码 (QR code), 点赞 (to like a post), 直播 (livestream) and 充值 (to top up) are all HSK 5 words in the 2025 syllabus.

The syllabus lists 处, 调 and 系 twice, once for each reading, and a few words such as 为 (wéi) appear again with a new use. This page shows each word once, so the table has 1,595 rows for the 1,600 syllabus entries.
