About HSK 3

HSK 3 doubles the vocabulary, adding 500 words to reach 1,000 in total. This is the level where learners start to handle everyday topics in some depth: school and work, travel, health, the weather and seasons, and their own opinions.

Several familiar characters come back with a new reading or meaning. 还 is read huán (to give back), 长 is read zhǎng (to grow), and 只 is read zhī as a measure word for animals.

The syllabus lists 得 twice, for dé (to get) and děi (to have to). This page shows it once with both readings, so the table has 499 rows.
