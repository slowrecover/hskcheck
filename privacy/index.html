Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

What the tools do with your text

Every tool on hskcheck.com runs in your browser. Text you paste into the level checker, words you search, and characters you put on a worksheet are processed on your own device. They are not sent to us or stored on any server.

Buying a printable pack

If you buy a printable pack, the order is handled by Creem (creem.io), our online reseller and merchant of record. Creem collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to take the payment, deliver the download and send your receipt, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details from Creem so we can help with your order and handle refunds. We never see your card number, and we don't send you marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show ads served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.

To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Analytics

We may use Google Search Console and similar tools to see which pages people find through search. These report aggregate figures, such as page views and search terms, not who you are.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The tools keep working without them.
